Welcome to RetroLemmini

Thank you for choosing RetroLemmini to play the classic games Lemmings™, Oh No! More Lemmings™, and Holiday Lemmings™.

This message appears the first time you start RetroLemmini. Please read the following:

RetroLemmini is developed by William James, based on SuperLemminiToo by Charles Irwin, SuperLemmini by Tsyu, and original Lemmini by Volker Oth. Many thanks to all developers for their hard work and contribution to this fantastic project, dedicated to keeping the classic Lemmings game available and accessible on modern computer systems!

Graphical assets modified from Lemmings for Windows 95, published by Dice Media and Psygnosis.
Original levels created by DMA Design (now Rockstar North).
